## Design rules (anti-generic)
- No purple or blue gradients, glassmorphism, glow or neon, emoji or sparkle decoration, blurred-blob heroes.
- No grid of identical cards, no cards nested in cards, no everything-centered layouts,
  no rounded-everything with big soft shadows.
- No filler copy ("Unlock insights", "Supercharge..."). Copy is plain, specific and short.
- Don't wrap a number in a decorative tile when a table, chart or inline figure reads better.
- Pro screens are data-dense: tables with tabular numerals, aligned columns, sticky headers, sortable columns.
- Show only data the backend returns. No invented numbers; design honest empty states.
- Keep the NgRx architecture: components read selectors and dispatch actions; no direct service calls.
- Do not commit unless I ask.
- Follow DESIGN.md for colors, type, spacing and components once it exists.
